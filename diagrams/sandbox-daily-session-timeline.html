Sandbox daily schedule

What the sandbox does automatically every day

Like a real broker, the sandbox squares off intraday (MIS) positions at each exchange's cut-off, snapshots the day's P&L, settles delivery trades overnight and starts a fresh session. Times are IST defaults; square-off times can be changed in sandbox settings.
