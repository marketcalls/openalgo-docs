AmiBroker limit orders

Your limit order chases the price until it fills or is cancelled

The AFL places a limit order near the current quote, keeps checking it, moves the price if it is still waiting, and cancels it after the last retry. Every request goes to OpenAlgo on your computer.

1
Place the orderYou press Buy or Sell. It reads bid and ask, sets the limit price from your price mode, rounds it to the tick size and sends a LIMIT order.
quotesplaceorder
2
Watch the orderAfter each retry delay it asks OpenAlgo for the order status and the average fill price.
orderstatus
3
Chase the priceStill open or pending with retries left: fetch a fresh bid and ask and move the limit price. Retry count goes up by one.
quotesmodifyorder
4
Cancel and check once moreWhen no retries are left or a price change fails: cancels the order, waits one retry delay and checks the status a final time. Your Cancel button starts this step at any time.
