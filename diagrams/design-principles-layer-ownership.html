Design principles

Each part has one job, and one central step decides where your order goes

A request is parsed and checked at the edge, then a service picks where the work happens: your broker, the sandbox, the database or a calculator. Logging and alerts ride along without ever blocking the order.

1. Entry pointReads the request, checks who you are, sets the HTTP status.
restx_api/blueprints/
2. ValidationRejects bad fields or an expired session before any work.
marshmallow schemacheck_session_validity
3. ServicePicks live or analyze mode, applies policy and runs the steps in order.
services/*_service.py
4. Where the work happens

Action Center queueSemi-auto mode: the order waits for your approval instead of executing.
5. Normalized responseSame OpenAlgo JSON shape whichever broker or mode did the work.
