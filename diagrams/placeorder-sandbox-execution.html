Analyzer mode · sandbox execution

The sandbox fills orders like a broker would, with virtual money

When analyzer mode is on, the order never reaches your broker. The sandbox validates it, blocks margin from your virtual funds, and fills it at once or on a later price tick, all inside sandbox.db.
