WebSocket and ZeroMQ

Every live update meets on one private bus

Market ticks from your broker and internal messages from the web app are all published to a single local ZeroMQ address. The WebSocket proxy is the only listener: it picks up everything and forwards each message to the signed-in clients that subscribed to it.
