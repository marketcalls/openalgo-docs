Python strategy hosting

Every strategy runs as its own separate program, on your schedule

You upload a script, OpenAlgo saves it, and a built-in scheduler starts and stops it in market hours. Each running strategy is its own process with its own log, and it places orders through the same OpenAlgo API as any other tool.
