Sandbox order lifecycle

From your order to a simulated fill

A sandbox order goes through the same checks as a live one. With analyzer mode on, the sandbox then checks the order and your virtual margin, saves it, and fills it at once if the price allows, or hands it to the background engine to fill on a later tick.
