Master contract download

Fresh symbols once a day, reused the rest of the time

When you log in (or press Force Download), OpenAlgo checks whether today's symbol list (after the 8 AM cutoff) is already saved. If not, it downloads it from your broker in the background, stores it and loads it into memory for fast order lookups.
