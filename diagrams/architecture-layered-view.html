How OpenAlgo is built

Every tool enters one guarded front door; only the last layer knows your broker

Charts, spreadsheets, scripts and AI agents all enter through the same guarded front door. The service layer does the work, and a broker plugin translates it for your broker, or the sandbox handles it in Analyzer mode.
